# Piano prayer v2

30-second original instrumental arrangement made for this project, 72 BPM.
The composition is newly sequenced; it is not a recording of an existing song.

Unlike the fully synthesized first version, this version uses recorded individual piano notes:

Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, licensed under CC BY 3.0.
Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Samples were pitch-shifted where needed, sequenced, filtered, mixed, and reverberated.
